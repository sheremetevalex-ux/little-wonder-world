# Little Wonder World ✨🦄👑

A magical, offline-capable iPhone PWA game collection designed specifically for a bright 4-year-old girl.

🌐 **Live URL:** [https://sheremetevalex-ux.github.io/little-wonder-world/](https://sheremetevalex-ux.github.io/little-wonder-world/)

## 📱 How to Install on iPhone (Full-Screen App Mode)

1. Open **Safari** on the iPhone and visit:  
   **`https://sheremetevalex-ux.github.io/little-wonder-world/`**
2. Tap the **Share** button (`📤` square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (`➕`).
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. Launch **Wonder World** directly from the iPhone Home Screen — it opens full-screen like a native iOS game and works **100% offline**!

## 🌟 Included Experiences

- **🌈 Wonder Island Hub:** Living interactive sky (Sunny Day, Golden Sunset, Starry Night), musical xylophone rainbow, custom dancing princess/fairy avatar, companion baby pet, **Surprise Wonder Egg Hatcher (`🥚✨`)**, and **24-Sticker Treasure Book (`📖`)**.

  - **Musical Bubble & Balloon Pop:** Floating soap bubbles with prize icons inside.
- **🦄 Cuddle Pet Spa & Care Salon:** 4 baby pets (Lumi Unicorn, Mochi Kitten, Puff Dragon, Pip Bunny) across 4 care rooms (Bubble Bath & Grooming, Yummy Kitchen Feeding, Cuddle Doctor Checkup, Cozy Bedtime & Lullaby).
- **🗺️ Enchanted Forest Quest:** 4 playable adventure challenges (Whispering Woods Hide-and-Seek, Rainbow River Lily Pad Pattern Crossing, Crystal Cave 3-Key Treasure Chest, and Sky Hot-Air Balloon Flight).
- **🧁 Sweet Treat Bakery:** Mix ingredients in the magic bowl, frost & decorate Party Cakes, Giant Cupcakes, and Unicorn Donuts, light & blow out birthday candles, and eat the cake bite-by-bite!
